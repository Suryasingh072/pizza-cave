Who We Are

Baked with Passion, Served with Pride

Pizza Cave began as a simple idea — bring restaurant-quality, wood-fired veg pizza to the neighbourhood, without the wait or the premium price tag. Tucked inside Goyal Complex, opposite BBD and near Crown Mall, Lucknow, our kitchen runs on fresh dough, quality cheese, and a genuine love for good food.

Every pizza that leaves our oven is hand-stretched, generously topped, and fired at high heat for that unmistakable crisp-yet-chewy base. From Classic Cheese to our bold Cave Special range, our 100% veg menu is designed around what our neighbourhood actually craves.

  • Fresh dough proofed daily, never frozen
  • 100% veg kitchen, quality-checked ingredients
  • Free home delivery, also on Swiggy & Zomato
  • Fast WhatsApp ordering, 30-minute delivery
